## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a tactile, after-hours social messaging experience crafted for night owls, party crews, and dynamic social circles. It reflects the atmosphere of a subterranean club or neon-drenched city strip: intimate, energetic, kinetic, and unapologetically alive.

### Style Movements & Visual Signature
- **Cyber-Nightlife Translucency:** Atmospheric deep-black surfaces layered with smoked obsidian glass, letting under-panel ambient glow bleed through subtly.
- **Luminescent Presence & State Aura:** Real-time state indicators do not sit passively; they pulse, glow, and cast tinted ambient luminescence across adjacent components.
- **Tactile Tactility:** Elements respond to touches with subtle spring scaling, glossy specular highlight lines, and active glow flashes that mirror tactile hardware interfaces.
- **Typographic Clash:** Technical, geometric display typography paired with razor-sharp modern UI sans for conversational speed and immediate readability.

## Colors

The color palette is built on an absolute nocturnal foundation to minimize eye fatigue during night usage while maximizing the pop of phosphorescent state colors.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#08080C` (Pure Nocturne Base)
- **Glass Surface Base:** `rgba(18, 18, 26, 0.75)` (Obsidian Smoked Glass)
- **Elevated Glass Surface:** `rgba(26, 26, 38, 0.85)`
- **Glass Border Stroke:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`
- **Specular Top Edge Highlight:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`

### Primary Brand Lighting
- **Electric Neon Purple:** `#A855F7`
- **Linear Gradient Accent:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #C084FC 0%, #9333EA 100%)`
- **Primary Radiant Glow:** `0 0 24px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.45)`
